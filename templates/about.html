Our Legacy

Established over 300 years ago, Shree Tatkalik Hanuman Mandir is renowned for its powerful deity who grants immediate relief to devotees. The temple's serene atmosphere and ancient rituals attract thousands of visitors daily.

"Tatkalik" means immediate in Sanskrit — Lord Hanuman here is believed to answer prayers instantly, making this a place of urgent hope and faith for countless devotees.
